# Testing M3 (Researcher pack), on desktop

## Lists

1. Open a section, for example **Research**. Under the page, find **Lists** and click **New list**.
2. Templates that suit the section come first. Pick **Papers**.
3. Type a title in **Add a paper** and press Enter. Add two or three.
4. Click a paper's title to open its panel. Fill in Authors, Year, a Link and a Rating. Everything saves as you go.
5. Switch to **Board**. Drag a card from **To read** to **Reading**. The view you pick is remembered.
6. Back in **Table**, click a column header to sort (click again for descending, a third time to clear). Change a status straight from the table.
7. **Fields & statuses**: rename the list, add a field (try **Choice** with a few options), rename or reorder fields, and rename a status. Items move with a renamed status. Removing a field only hides it; the values stay.
8. **Ctrl+K** finds lists by name and items by any text in them (title, authors, notes).
9. **⋯ → Delete list** moves the list and its items to Trash; **Restore** brings both back.

## Conference deadlines in AoE

1. New list → **Conference deadlines**. Add one, for example "CoRL 2027".
2. Open it and set **Paper deadline**. The time starts at 23:59 and the zone at **AoE** (Anywhere on Earth); you can change both.
3. Under it you'll see **Your time** (for example 23:59 AoE on the 24th is 17:29 on the 25th in India) and how long is left.
4. **Today → Coming up** lists deadlines in the next 7 days with your time and the AoE time. Click one to open it.
5. **Calendar** shows it at your local time. Click it to open the item.
6. Set its status to **Submitted** (or **Skipped**): it leaves Coming up and the calendar. Which statuses count as done is set in **Fields & statuses**.

## Adviser meetings

1. Open **Adviser Meetings** (or any section) and click **New meeting**. It opens for the current hour; the title is your last meeting's title.
2. Set the title, date, time and who it's with. Write an agenda and notes (notes work like section pages: "/" for headings, lists and checklists).
3. Add action items in plain language, e.g. "Read RT-2 fri". They're ordinary tasks: they show up in All Tasks and on Today when due.
4. The flag on an action item means "bring this up next time". Tick items off as you finish them.
5. Make another meeting a week later (**New meeting**, then set its date). **From earlier meetings** shows every item still open, plus the items you flagged at the meeting before, even if done.
6. From the **Calendar**: click a meeting event (a weekly one works best) and choose **Meeting notes**. Each occurrence gets its own notes; opening it again returns the same one.
7. Meetings you create here show on the calendar too. **Ctrl+K** finds meetings by title, notes, agenda or who was there.
8. **⋯ → Delete meeting** moves the notes to Trash. Its action items stay in your tasks.

## Courses

1. Open a course, e.g. **SMAI** (inside Coursework). **New list**: **Assessments** is suggested first.
2. Add assignments, quizzes and exams. Open one to set its Type, Due (date and time), Weight %, Score and Out of.
3. Above the table you see your grade so far, e.g. "So far: 8 of 10 marks graded (80%)", and a note if the weights don't add up to 100%.
4. Due dates show on the calendar and in Coming up until you mark them Submitted or Graded.

## People

1. **People** is in the sidebar. Add someone, and pick their **Relation** (Family, Partner, Friends, Adviser & lab…) in the table.
2. Open them to type a **Birthday** or **Anniversary** (with the real year), phone, email, gift ideas and notes.
3. Birthdays and anniversaries come round every year: they show on the calendar and in Today's Coming up the week before.
4. **Board** groups people by relation. In a meeting's **With** field, names from People are suggested as you type.

## Files (attachments)

1. Open a task, a list item or a meeting. Under **Files**, click **Attach file** or drop files onto that area.
2. Click a file to open it (PDFs and images open in a tab; other files download). The × moves it to Trash.
3. Files are kept on this device only, up to 25 MB each. JSON backups don't include them yet, and they won't sync until M5. For big files, paste a cloud-drive link in the notes instead.

## Focus sessions

1. Open **Focus** in the sidebar. Pick 25, 50 or 90 minutes (or type a length) and, if you like, a task. **Start**.
2. Or open any task and click **Focus 25 min**.
3. While it runs, the countdown shows next to **Focus** in the sidebar on every page. It keeps going if you reload or close the app.
4. When time is up you get a message (and a system notification if you allowed them in Settings and the app is in the background). The session is saved.
5. **Stop and save** ends early and keeps the minutes you worked; **Discard** throws it away; **+5 min** adds time.
6. Below: deep work today and this week, the last 7 days as bars, this week by section, and recent sessions (× moves one to Trash).
