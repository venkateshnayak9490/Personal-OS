# Testing M4 (Analytics, Reviews & Life modules), on desktop

## Analytics

1. Complete a few tasks, skip one (⋯ → Skip…), and leave one with a past due date open. Do a focus session and tick a habit.
2. Open **Analytics** (sidebar, under Tools). Pick **Week**, **Month** or **Year**; the arrows move between periods.
3. The four numbers:
   - **Completed**: tasks you ticked in the period.
   - **Completion rate**: completed ÷ (completed + skipped + missed). Missed means still open with a due date in the period that has passed; something due today only counts once the day is over. Cancelled tasks are left out.
   - **Deep work**: focus-session time.
   - **Habits**: done ÷ what the habit asked for. Rest days don't count against you, and neither does the current week of an "n× a week" habit.
4. **Tasks completed per day** (per month for a year): hover a bar for its number.
5. **Last 12 months**: one square per day, darker means more done. Hover a square for the date and count.
6. **By section**: click a section to see only its numbers; click again to go deeper. The trail at the top (Everything › Work › Research) takes you back.

## Reviews

1. Open **Reviews** (sidebar, under Tools). Pick **Day**, **Week**, **Month** or **Year**.
2. The top shows the numbers so far and, under "What you completed", the tasks you finished.
3. Answer the questions and **Save review**. Saving freezes the numbers: later edits to tasks don't rewrite an old review.
4. Past reviews are listed below; click one to open it.
5. On the last day of the week and the first day of the next, Today shows "Take five minutes for your weekly review" until you've written it.

## Notes

1. Open **Notes** (sidebar, under Private). **New note**, give it a title (or leave it: the first line names it), and write. "/" gives headings, lists and checklists.
2. Pin a note to keep it on top. Pick a section if it belongs somewhere.
3. Filter the list on the left, or press **Ctrl+K** to search the text of every note.
4. The bin icon moves a note to Trash.

## Journal

1. Open **Journal**. It opens on today; the arrows and the date button move between days (not into the future).
2. Pick a mood (Rough … Great) and write. One page per day.
3. Tick **Private** to keep an entry out of search, the "Earlier entries" list (it shows only "Private") and the Trash name. It is not encrypted yet; that comes with the Vault (M5).
4. **Delete entry** moves the day to Trash; writing on that day again brings it back.

## Thesis

1. Open the **Thesis** section. Add chapters (e.g. Introduction, Related work) and set each one's word goal.
2. **Log writing**: pick a chapter and type its word count now (what your editor shows), plus minutes if you like. The change since last time is worked out for you; cutting words shows as a minus.
3. The top line shows total words, % of the goal and this week's change. A chapter moves from Outline to Drafting on its first words; change its status any time.
4. **Writing log** (fold-out) lists recent sessions.

## Workouts

1. Open **Gym & Fitness** (inside Self-Improvement) and click **Log a workout**.
2. Type the exercise (earlier ones are suggested), reps and weight (leave weight empty for bodyweight), then **Add set**. The exercise stays filled in for the next set.
3. A set that beats your best (heavier, or a higher estimated one-rep max) gets a **PR** badge and a message. Your first set of an exercise is only the baseline.
4. Back on the section: recent workouts with their PR count, and your best lifts.

## Daily check-in

1. On **Today**, the **Check-in** card: mood, energy, hours of sleep, glasses of water (+/−) and weight. All optional, saved as you tap.
2. Mood is the same as the journal's mood for the day.
3. The **Health** section shows today's check-in, the last 14 days and the average sleep and energy.
4. Customize Today can hide or move the card.
