# Testing M5 (Accounts, Sync & Security), on desktop

## Vault

1. Open **Vault** (sidebar, under Private). Choose a passphrase (at least 10 characters; four or five unrelated words work well) and type it twice. **Create Vault** takes about a second: that slowness is what makes guessing hard.
2. You get a **recovery key** (8 groups of 4). Copy it or download it as a file and keep it somewhere outside this app. It's shown only once. Tick "I've saved my recovery key" to continue.
3. **New item**: give it a title, write private details, and **Add file** (ID scans, certificates; up to 25 MB). Everything is encrypted before it's stored. The search box looks inside items while unlocked.
4. **Lock** (top right) closes it. The Vault also locks after 5 minutes without a tap or key press, and whenever you reload or close the app.
5. Unlock with the passphrase. A wrong one is refused.
6. **Forgot the passphrase?** Enter the recovery key (dashes and capitals don't matter) and a new passphrase. Nothing is re-encrypted; the recovery key keeps working.
7. **Change passphrase** is under the item list.
8. If you forget the passphrase **and** lose the recovery key, the Vault can't be opened by anyone. That's by design.

Backups include the Vault as ciphertext; encrypted files stay on this device (like other attachments) until sync.

## Private journal entries

1. In **Journal**, ticking **Private** without a Vault explains that you need to set one up first.
2. With the Vault unlocked, a private entry is encrypted with the Vault key. Its earlier text is also removed from the history.
3. After a reload (or auto-lock), a private day shows "This entry is private. Unlock the Vault to read and write it." with a passphrase box right there.
4. Private entries never appear in search, the "Earlier entries" list text, or Trash names.

## App PIN lock

1. **Settings → App lock → Set a PIN** (4–8 digits, twice).
2. Reload: the app asks for the PIN before showing anything. After 5 wrong tries it makes you wait (30 s, then longer).
3. **Lock again**: right away, or after 1, 5 or 15 minutes away from the app. **Lock now** locks it (and the Vault) immediately.
4. **Forgot your PIN?** If you have a Vault, its passphrase removes the PIN. Without a Vault, the only way back in is clearing this site's data in the browser (restore from a backup afterwards).
5. The PIN is a privacy screen for this device. It doesn't encrypt anything and isn't synced.

## Also fixed

Analytics, Reviews, Notes, Journal and Vault are now in the sidebar (under **Tools** and **Private**) and on the phone's **More** page. Before, they were only reachable by address or Ctrl+K.

## Sync

Needs your Supabase project first: follow [docs/setup/SUPABASE.md](../setup/SUPABASE.md). Until then, **Settings → Account & sync** says sync isn't switched on, and everything works on this device as before.

Once it's on:

1. **Settings → Account & sync**: enter your email, open the link from the email on the same device. Everything here uploads.
2. On a second device, use **"Already use Personal OS on another device? Sign in"** on the welcome screen. Your sections, tasks and everything else arrive; no starter sections are added.
3. Edit different things on both devices (a task's title on one, its due date on the other). Both changes survive.
4. Edit the same note's text on both while one is offline. The newer text wins, and the other is kept as a pinned note "Conflict copy: …".
5. Delete something forever on one device: it disappears on the other too.
6. Status shows "Synced 1 min ago"; **Sync now** forces it. It also syncs on start, when you come back online, every minute, and a few seconds after edits.
7. **Sign out** keeps this device's data. **Delete my cloud data** removes everything on the server for your account (devices keep their copies).

## Fixed: production security headers

The site's security policy (vercel.json) blocked WebAssembly and outside connections. That broke **paper import (since M4)** and would have broken the Vault, PIN lock and sync on the live site. It now allows exactly: WebAssembly, Supabase, Crossref and DataCite. Local test builds now send the same headers, so this is caught by the tests.
